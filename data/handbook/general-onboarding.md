# General Onboarding (New Hire Checklist)

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/general-onboarding/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Overview

Onboarding is incredibly important at GitLab. We don't expect you to hit the ground running from day one.

**We highly recommend taking at least two full weeks for onboarding and only in week three starting with team-specific onboarding and training.** Feel free to participate in your team's work in your first two weeks, but don't feel you have to contribute heavily.

All onboarding steps are in the onboarding issue template owned by the People Operations team (see `gitlab-com/people-group/people-operations/employment-templates`). The onboarding process for the new team member is self-driven, self-learning, and as asynchronous as possible to help settle into the remote life at GitLab.

At GitLab we dogfood our own product — all onboarding tasks are completed in a GitLab issue.

## Timeline

- **At least 4 days prior to hire date:** The People Operations Specialist assigned to the team member opens the onboarding issue.
- **Day 2 of onboarding:** An Access Request is generated (if a template exists for the role). Access requests are owned by the IT team. Questions to `#it-help` in Slack.
- **Main section due date:** 30 days.
- **Employment bot automatically closes** any onboarding issues still open after **60 days**.

In certain instances (national holiday, Family and Friends Day), People Operations may not be able to assist with onboardings. These dates are documented in the People Operations team availability.

## TaNewKi Welcome Call

The People Operations team hosts a **pre-onboarding call** (the "Ta-New-Ki" call — a play on Tanuki, the Japanese raccoon dog in our logo). The purpose is to give onboarding team members a chance to meet, socialize, and ask questions **before** their start date. Internally this is referred to as an AMA call.

- Hiring managers and current team members (e.g., onboarding buddies) are welcome to join.
- Occurs every two weeks on Thursday at three time slots to accommodate timezones:
  - **4:00 PM PT**
  - **12:00 AM PT**
  - **8:30 AM PT**
- Takes place **a week or two before actual start date**.

Agenda: review onboarding process on Day 1, encourage taking time, self-driven / self-service emphasis, Q&A.

## Slack Channels (All New Team Members Auto-Added)

By default, all new team members are added to these Slack channels from day 1:

- `#new_team_members` — go introduce yourself
- `#diversity_inclusion_and_belonging`
- `#it_help` — IT support (laptop, 1Password, Okta, JAMF, etc.)
- `#questions` — anything the Handbook can't answer
- `#donut-be-strangers` — coffee chat setup
- `#team-member-updates` — new team members, anniversaries, goodbyes
- `#thanks` — recognition/thanks
- `#whats-happening-at-gitlab` — company updates and notifications

## Managers of New Team Members

- Issue is created at least **4 business days prior to start date**.
- Manager and People Operations Team member are assigned.
- Both have tasks that need to be completed **prior to start date** for a smooth onboarding.

### Compliance

The Senior People Operations Specialist runs a **monthly audit** of all open onboarding issues to ensure the new team member, manager, and People Ops tasks are completed — especially tasks tied to company compliance (security, payroll, etc.). Outstanding tasks trigger a ping. The employment bot closes any issue open after **60 days**.

### Completing the Onboarding Issue

To ensure successful completion, all tasks must be checked off. Checking a box indicates one of:
- I have completed this task
- I have checked and this task is not applicable to me

## Temporary Service Provider (Contractor / Consultant) Onboarding

The People team is **not involved** in hiring, onboarding, or offboarding of temporary service providers. Work with the Procurement team; the Temporary Service Provider internal handbook page has templates and helpful information.

## Buddy System

The **Onboarding Buddy** program pairs each new hire with an existing team member to provide social support, answer newbie questions, and help ease the onboarding experience. A Merge Request Buddy separately helps with code/MR review culture. Reach out to your onboarding buddy or to other GitLab team members for help with any onboarding tasks.

## Access Provisioning

Through onboarding issues, new team members gain access to baseline entitlements. **On Day 2**, an Access Request is generated if a template exists for the role. Access requests are owned by the IT team. Direct any questions to `#it-help` in Slack.

## Remote-specific Onboarding Guidance

GitLab recommends the onboarding process take **at least two full weeks**, with the third week designated for team-specific onboarding and training. Onboarding has three dimensions: organizational (logistics, tools, handbook), technical (using tools, early wins), and social (onboarding buddy, Slack intros, coffee chats).
