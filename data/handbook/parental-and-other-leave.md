# Parental Leave, Emergency Leave, Military Leave, Equity Vesting During Leave

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/leave-types/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## GitLab Parental Leave

GitLab's paid Parental Leave is available for any active team member **regardless of gender** who becomes a parent through childbirth or adoption while working at GitLab. Different countries or US states may provide team members with their own statutory Parental Leave benefits.

### Eligibility, Entitlement, Planning

- **Available from your first day of employment**, provided your child was born or newly adopted during your employment at GitLab.
- **16 weeks of paid Parental Leave**, which must be used prior to the child's **first birthday or the first anniversary of adoption**.
- Leave may be taken all at once or split into **up to three segments**.
- **Calculation formula:** 1 week = 7 days. If you return on a Monday, your leave ends on Sunday.
- **If your Parental Leave falls within the 4 scheduled Family and Friends Days, parental leave is extended by 4 days.**
- Unused public holidays will not rollover to the next calendar year while on parental leave (unless statutorily required).

### No Work Required During Your Leave

- You are **not required** to log into any GitLab systems, review or approve compensation documents, or respond to any work-related emails or deadlines.
- Disregard automated system emails during leave; approvals are handled by the team or upon your return.
- Any compensation components will be preserved and addressed appropriately upon your return.

### Supplement with Paid Time Off

After Parental Leave, you may use Flexible PTO with manager coordination (following the 2× notice rule). Submit PTO in Workday under "Paid Time Off" and notify Absence Management.

**You cannot extend paid parental leave using Paid Caregiver Sick Time unless the family member has a serious health condition.**

### How to Initiate Your Leave

- **Share when comfortable.** Recommend communicating **2–3 months before** leave starts for workload planning.
- **30–45 days prior to leave**, submit your request per your location:
  - **USA and Canada:** Submit to **Tilt** (accessed through Okta). Personalized leave plan is built within **2–5 business days**. Contact: `LeaveSupport@ourtilt.com` or text **(970) 449-0178**.
  - **All other locations:** Enter Parental Leave in Workday under the Leaves dropdown → "GitLab Parental Leave". Absence Management will email within **48 hours**.
- **You cannot edit dates yourself** after approval — contact Absence Management for changes.
- Provide **proof of birth / relationship and/or adoption within 15 calendar days** from leave start. Email docs to `leaves@gitlab.com`.

### Pay While on Parental Leave

- **100% of weekly wages** through the GitLab Paid Parental Leave program.
- Where statutory leave pay is available in your country or US state, you are **required** to apply for it; GitLab supplements to equal 100% of wages.
- You will not receive more than 100% of your wages combined.

### Returning to Work

- About a week before your return, Absence Management sends a welcome-back email asking you to confirm your return date.
- **Returning at 50% capacity is available** — standard guideline is **2–4 weeks at reduced capacity**, flexible. Options:
  - Adjusting workload to ramp up gradually
  - Adjusting working days/hours (4×10 days, early/late shifts, etc.)
  - Gradually increasing working days (2 days week 1, 3 days week 2, etc.)
- This is NOT tracked in Workday and does not require Absence Management review.

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave provides paid time away from work to use in unexpected scenarios such as natural disasters, community emergencies, or any other catastrophe. Available from **first day of work**.

### Request Process

1. Notify your manager as soon as possible (share only what you're comfortable with). You can also email `leaves@gitlab.com`.
2. Document in Workday: "Leaves" → "Emergency Leave" → Submit.
3. Absence Management contacts you within **48 hours** to confirm dates.

See also the People Group Incident Response Plan for what the People Group does when team members are impacted by an emergency event.

## Military Leave

GitLab protects the rights of team members absent for military leave. No discrimination or retaliation on the basis of uniformed-service membership.

- **Leaves ≤ 31 days:** No changes to job, pay, or benefits. Team member returns on next scheduled shift.
- **Leaves > 31 days:** Up to **6 months unpaid leave** (subject to statutory entitlement and business needs). Beyond 6 months the member is offboarded but eligible for reinstatement.
- **Healthcare:** May retain for up to **2 years or end of service** (whichever first), at own expense. Otherwise benefits end on **day 31**.
- **Stock vesting** continues up to 6 months of unpaid military leave.

**Reinstatement:** Up to **2 years** after service ends (2 years for disabilities). Required: proper notice, honorable release, and application within:
- **< 31 days:** report on next regularly scheduled work period
- **31–180 days:** apply within **14 days**
- **> 180 days:** apply within **90 days**

The "escalator principle" applies — the team member returns to the position they would have attained if they had not been called to serve.

**Applying:** Provide **30 days advance notice** via Workday → Leaves → "Military Leave". Documentation may be requested.

## Sick Leave

Sick absences **6 days or longer** are considered a leave of absence. See the Sick Time policy in the Time Off Types handbook page.

## Caregiving Sick Leave

Caregiver sick absences **6 days or longer** are considered a leave of absence.

## Equity Vesting During Leave

- Stock vesting is **paused after six months of continuous leave** and resumes on your first day back at work.
- Applied in accordance with applicable local laws and statutory requirements; may not apply in all locations or leave types.
- For details, see the Equity policy page. Questions to the Stock Admin team via HelpLab.
