# Total Rewards: Compensation, Bonuses, Equity

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/compensation/ and https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/stock-options/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Compensation Philosophy

GitLab's compensation philosophy is to attract and reward team members who deliver meaningful results through collaboration while operating with efficiency as GitLab grows.

The standardized framework uses local labor market data to ensure fair and competitive compensation with a focus on transparency. It aligns compensation with pay for performance — through individual excellence and collaborative efforts.

## Compensation Elements

Total compensation at GitLab combines three components:

### 1. Base Salary

Base salary is annual guaranteed pay, typically paid in local currency. It's determined by **role, level, and location**.

GitLab sets base salary ranges using market data from compensation surveys (e.g., **Radford**), targeting compensation at or above the **50th percentile of the software industry**.

### 2. Variable Pay

Some roles include variable pay in addition to base salary. Variable pay has a target amount but is not guaranteed.

#### Director+ Bonuses

Directors and above (excluding those on a sales commission plan) have a bonus target that's a **percentage of base salary** and **paid twice per year (semi-annually)** based on company performance.

- Example: 15% of a $100,000 base salary = **$15,000 annual bonus target**.

#### Sales Commissions

Sales roles are structured as **On-Target Earnings (OTE)** = base salary + commission target. The split varies by role. Commission is tied to **quota achievement**:
- Hitting quota → full target commission
- Exceeding quota → upside
- Missing quota → lower commission

- Example: $100,000 OTE with a 60/40 split = **$60,000 base + $40,000 commission target**.

**Total Target Cash (TTC)** = base + variable target. For sales roles, often called OTE. TTC/OTE is what a team member would receive if **100% of targets are met**.

### 3. Equity (RSUs)

Equity compensation provides ownership in GitLab's future success through **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)**. Shares vest over time. RSU value is tied to GitLab's stock price at sale.

Equity grants are given at:
- **New hire grants** as part of offer packages
- **Refresh grants** through Annual Compensation Review (performance + growth potential)
- **Promotion grants** when advancing to a new level

## When Compensation is Determined

### Time of Hire

Initial compensation package is determined when you join. Offers are based on:
- Market range for the role and level
- Location of the role
- Experience and skills as they relate to the role

### Annual Compensation Review (ACR)

- Company-wide review of all team member compensation, once per year.
- **Effective Date: May 1**
- Addresses market changes, recognizes performance, ensures internal equity.

## Equity: RSU Details

Since September 2021, GitLab has issued **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)** for grants. GitLab is **no longer giving stock option grants**.

- RSUs are subject to forfeiture and are expected to **vest over 2–4 years** on a combination of **bi-annual and quarterly** basis.
- Governed by the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan.

### Sell-to-Cover (RSUs)

Shares are sold to pay taxes due on RSU vesting. Automatic sale based on your country's tax rate.

Example: if 100 shares vest and tax rate is 30%, 30 shares are sold to cover tax; 70 shares land in your E*TRADE account.

For US team members, withholding at vest uses federal supplemental tax rate + state/local + FICA + Social Security.

### Departure / Termination

If a team member leaves GitLab:
- **90 days post-termination to exercise any vested options.**
- **Unvested RSUs and options are forfeited** upon exit date.

## Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

The 2021 ESPP enables eligible team members to purchase Class A common stock via payroll deductions with:
- **15% purchase price discount** of fair market value on the IPO date or purchase date, whichever is lower.
- **24-month look-back** with **four purchase dates**: **May and November** of each year.
- First purchase occurred May 2022.

### Eligibility

1. Employed via a GitLab entity with **50+ headcount**.
2. Works more than **20 hours per week**.
3. Actively employed at the beginning of the Offering Period to enroll.
4. Actively employed on the last date of the Purchase Period to purchase.

Eligible ESPP GitLab entities include:
- GitLab Canada Corp.
- GitLab GmbH (Germany)
- GitLab B.V. (Netherlands/Belgium/Finland)
- GitLab Inc. (US)
- GitLab Federal, LLC
- GitLab UK Limited
- GitLab Pty Ltd (Australia/New Zealand)
- GitLab Ireland Limited
- GitLab France S.A.S. (from 2025-06-01)
- GitLab GK (Japan) (from 2025-12-01)
- GitLab India Private Limited (from 2025-12-01)

### Contribution Changes

- **Increases are NOT allowed** during Offering Period.
- **Decreases**: allowed once during any Offering Period.
- Team members may reduce contribution to **0%** during an offering period; prior contributions still purchase shares on scheduled purchase date.
