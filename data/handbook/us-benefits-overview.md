# GitLab Inc (US) Benefits Overview

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/inc-benefits-us/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Getting Started with Your Benefits

- **You have 30 days from your start date** to sign up for benefits through **PlanSource** (GitLab's benefits system).
- If you don't choose a plan within 30 days, you'll automatically get the **Medical Waiver Allowance** and won't have other optional coverage.

### Who Can Get Benefits?

You're eligible if you're a full-time GitLab team member working **at least 30 hours per week**. Benefits start on your first day.

You can also cover:
- Spouse or domestic partner
- Children up to age **26** (including adopted and stepchildren)
- Adult children with disabilities who can't support themselves

If you and an eligible family member both work at GitLab, you can only be covered once under the plans. Domestic partner coverage may be taxable income.

### How to Enroll

1. Login to **Workday** (`https://www.myworkday.com/gitlab/d/home.htmld`)
2. Click **View All Apps** → **Personal Information** app
3. Scroll to **External Links** → select **US Benefits Enrollment - PlanSource**

### Qualifying Life Events (change outside open enrollment)

- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Spouse's employment changes
- Moving to a different state

**Log into PlanSource within 30 days** of the qualifying event and submit changes with required documentation.

## Medical Coverage

GitLab provides medical insurance through **Cigna** nationwide, with **Kaiser** options available for California and Hawaii residents. Opt out if you have coverage elsewhere.

### Medical Waiver Allowance

Already have group medical coverage? Opt out and receive a **$150 monthly benefit allowance** *(was $300 in 2025)* while still enrolling in dental, vision, and other benefits. You must actively waive medical coverage in PlanSource to receive this allowance.

### Cigna Medical Plans (three options)

1. **Cigna EPO** — Configured as PPO with limited out-of-network benefits ($10k/$20k deductible, $20k/$40k out-of-pocket max, 50% coinsurance out-of-network).
2. **Cigna HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan)** — Eligible for HSA contributions.
3. **Cigna PPO** — Standard PPO. Utah members get extended PPO network including Intermountain Healthcare System.

**GitLab HSA Contributions (HDHP enrollees):** $100/mo individual, $125/mo family. HSA contributions are taxable at the state level in California, Alabama, and New Jersey.

### Kaiser Medical Plans

Available in Hawaii and California. Kaiser claims window: **365 days** for out-of-network.

## Dental & Vision Coverage

- **Dental** through Cigna: DPPO Base Plan and DPPO Buy Up Plan.
- **Vision** through Cigna + EyeMed: Vision Base and Vision Buy Up.

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Through **New York Life Group Benefits** — no cost:
- **Behavioral Health:** 3 face-to-face sessions with counselors
- **Legal Services:** Free 30-minute consultations and 25% fee discounts
- **Financial Consultations:** Free 30-minute consultation and 25% tax services discount
- **Health Advocacy** and **Travel Protection** also included

## Life Insurance & AD&D

### Basic Life Insurance & AD&D (company-paid)

- **2× annual base salary** in Basic Life Insurance
- **Maximum benefit: $500,000**
- Equal AD&D coverage through Cigna / New York Life
- Automatic coverage from day one, no cost

### Supplemental Life Insurance

Additional voluntary coverage through Cigna. If you choose supplemental above the guaranteed amount, you (or dependent) may need to complete an Evidence of Insurability form.

Supplemental life insurance is **not** included in COBRA and ends on your last day.

## Disability Protection

- **Short-term and long-term disability insurance provided at no cost.**
- Replaces **up to 66.7% of base salary** for qualifying disabilities.
- Short-term disability benefit maximum period: **26 weeks**.
- Contact Absence Management at `leaves@gitlab.com` to apply.

**Claim process (NYLife):**
1. Submit Short-Term Disability application.
2. Within **3 business days**, NYLife contacts you for additional info if needed.
3. Decision.
4. Absence Management files all paperwork in Workday.

## 401(k) Plan with Company Match

- **Immediate eligibility and vesting.**
- **Company Match: 50% of contributions on the first 6% of earnings** (up to **$1,500 annually**).
- Investment options through **Fidelity NetBenefits**.
- Phone support: **Fidelity at 800-835-5097**.

### 401(k) Plan Features

- **After-tax contributions** with automatic Roth in-plan conversions available.
- **In-plan Roth conversion** (pre-tax source) — converted amounts taxable in year of conversion.
- **Rollovers** in or out via Fidelity forms.
- **401(k) Loans:** up to **50% of vested balance or $50,000**, whichever is less, at **Prime + 1% interest**.
- **401(k) Committee:** Chief People Officer, Principal Accounting Officer, Director of Total Rewards, Director Treasurer, Chief Legal Officer (observer).

## Flexible Spending & Health Savings Accounts

### Health Savings Account (HSA)

- GitLab monthly contribution: **$100 individual / $125 family**.
- Must be enrolled in the **HDHP** to be eligible.
- **Pre-tax** money for qualified medical expenses.
- Money rolls over annually and never expires.
- Invest balance in mutual funds, stocks, bonds once minimum balance is reached.
- At **age 65**, withdraw for any purpose without penalty (medical expenses remain tax-free).
- Transfer from a previous HSA: contact **WEX (Discovery)** at **866.451.3399** or `customerservice@discoverybenefits.com`.
- HSA stays with you forever after leaving GitLab (you become responsible for monthly admin fee).

## ID Cards & Access

- **Cigna:** Digital cards via myCigna website and mobile app.
- **Kaiser:** Physical cards mailed within **3 weeks**; digital cards via Kaiser online portal and mobile app.

## Medicare & Tax Documents

- GitLab plans are **Medicare-creditable**.
- Visit `medicare.gov` or call **1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)**.
- **1095-C Form:** GitLab no longer automatically mails paper 1095-C forms (Paperwork Burden Reduction Act, Dec 2024). Access it via PlanSource → Documents → ACA Documents. Available no later than **March 2nd** of the following year.
