# Spending Company Money (Expenses & Reimbursement)

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/spending-company-money/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

**All team members and contractors must protect our Company assets, such as equipment, supplies, cash, and information. Treat Company assets with the same care you would if they were your own. No team member or contractor may commit theft, fraud or embezzlement, or misuse Company property.**

## Guidelines

1. As a public company, GitLab has an obligation to act in the best interests of the corporation and its shareholders. This obligation is shared by all team members and is the basis of our global travel and expense policy; team members must be thoughtful in spending the Company's money.
2. Spending Company money includes travel and expenses.
3. You may privately use GitLab property, a MacBook for example, to check your private e-mails or watch a movie as long as it does not violate the law, harm GitLab, or interfere with Intellectual Property. More details are in the Internal Acceptable Use Policy (`/handbook/people-group/acceptable-use-policy`).
4. **File your expense report in a timely manner; expense claims over 90 days from purchase date will not be reimbursed unless approved by your Manager.** The sooner it is submitted, the sooner you will get reimbursement.
5. If team members knowingly submit expenses for reimbursement for personal purchases that are not covered in the expense policy, this is a violation of the GitLab Code of Business Conduct & Ethics.

## Ways to spend company money

1. **Individual expenses on a personal credit card** — see the Global Travel and Expense Policy for what's reimbursable and how to submit.
2. **Individual expenses using a virtual card** — see the Global Travel and Expense Policy.
3. **Individual expenses using a Navan physical card** — see the Navan Physical Card Policy.
4. **Company expenses using a Coupa virtual card** — see the Coupa Virtual Card guide under Enterprise Applications.
5. **Company expenses using a purchase order** — see the Procurement Process.

## Expense tooling

- **Navan** is GitLab's expense tool used for personal expense reimbursement and for issuing virtual cards for approved one-time purchases.
- **Coupa** is used for purchase orders (POs) and vendor payments.
- **Zip** is the procurement intake tool where purchase requests are submitted, reviewed, and approved.

## Submission deadline

- Expense claims **over 90 days from the purchase date will not be reimbursed** without Manager approval.
- Receipts must be uploaded into Navan within **30 days of the transaction date** for corporate card usage.
- US team members using corporate cards need receipts for any single charge **over $50**; other jurisdictions require receipts for every charge.
- A Missing Receipt Affidavit (PDF) must be submitted if a receipt is lost.

## Notes on tax and reimbursement limits

- The Global Travel and Expense Policy (detailed limits, per-diem amounts, meal caps) now lives in the GitLab **internal handbook** at `https://internal.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/expenses/` and is only accessible to active GitLab team members.
- For home office and equipment expenses, see the Equipment section of the internal handbook's Expenses page, or submit via Navan. Questions go to `expenses@gitlab.com` or HelpLab.
