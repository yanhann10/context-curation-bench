# Navan Purchasing Card Policy (Corporate Credit Card)

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/accounts-payable/corp-credit-cards/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## 1. Policy

- The Navan Purchasing Card policy facilitates lower-cost purchases necessary for company operations.
- It does **not** override GitLab's Procurement Policy — it provides an alternative for lower-cost goods and services.
- Cards are used for appropriate business purposes with regular controls.

**This policy applies to:**
- Ad hoc vendors spending **less than $5,000 USD annually**.
- Transactions where the vendor only accepts credit card payments.

**The policy restricts card use:**
- Cards **cannot** be used to bypass the GitLab Procurement process.
- **No charges above $5,000 USD annually per vendor** — these must go through Zip for procurement processing.
- **No items already noted as non-permissible in the Expense policy (e.g., software)**.
- No items listed as "non-permissible" below.

Cards are only provided to team members under their own name, with the request/approval of the Direct Manager and the VP Accounting Operation's final approval. Cards/card numbers **must not be shared** among team members.

## 2. General Guidelines

- **All card charges require a receipt.** US team members require receipts only for anything **over $50**. Receipts must be uploaded into Navan within **30 days** of the transaction date.
- If a receipt is missing, a **Missing Receipt Affidavit** must be completed and attached (as a PDF) to the expense.
- Use the appropriate Expense type for proper allocation. If not listed, use "Other" with supporting details in the description.
- Add expense tags under "Classifications" where applicable (e.g. company-wide events, marketing campaigns, professional services).
- Non-Permissible items will be flagged upon charge.
- **Single transactions must not exceed $5,000 USD.** For any purchase over $5,000, enter a Purchase Requisition in Zip.
- If a charge is made in error, raise a HelpLab ticket with the Expense admin team. Avoid charges in error.

## 3. Permissible Items

### Team Building Activities / Off-Site / Event Costs

- Registration fees for team building and off-site events, dinners, deposits for activities, and room rentals (all **under $5,000 USD**).
- **Team meals of up to 20 people**, not to exceed **$100 per person**.

### Gifts for Significant Life Events

- **Up to $125 USD per gift** for significant life events.
- Managers may send gifts and flowers on behalf of team members (e.g., well wishes before surgery or for loss of a loved one). **Baby gifts are not reimbursable** (GitLab already sends them a gift).
- Executive Business Assistants may purchase gifts for team members on behalf of managers; an email or Slack pre-approval from the manager must be attached.
- **Gift cards for team members are not allowed** for these gifts.
- Expenses are tracked by department; the submitter must be in the same department as the recipient.

### Shipping & Postage Fees

- Shipping for event items, prizes, gifts for significant life events.
- SWAG shipping follows the merchandise workflow in the Marketing handbook.

## 4. Non-Permissible Items

Any purchase **over $5,000 USD** (or equivalent local currency) is not permissible. Items below must be expensed personally through normal reimbursement or procurement:

### Airfare and related costs
- Airfare for an individual team member must be purchased through Navan.
- Includes in-flight purchases, seat upgrades, wifi, baggage costs, and meals.
- Personal expenses follow the normal expense policy.

### Hotels, Transportation & Related Travel Expenses
- Not permitted for individual team members on the Navan Purchasing Card.
- Examples: meals, wifi, Global Entry / passport charges, car rental, mileage, Uber/Lyft for personal use.
- These must be expensed via the employee reimbursement process.

### Co-working Space
- Not permitted for individual team members — must run through the normal expense reimbursement process.
- Only permitted if the space is used for an event.

### Software
- **All software purchases are non-permissible** on the Navan Purchasing Card (see Individual Use Software policy — purchases must go through the virtual-card workflow after Security/Privacy review).

### Other non-permissible
- Personal items of any kind
- Fines, penalties, or legal fees not incurred as GitLab business
- Cash advances
- Any item or service deemed inappropriate for corporate spend
