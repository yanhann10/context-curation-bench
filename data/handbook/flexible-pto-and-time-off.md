# Flexible PTO, Public Holidays, Sick Time, Bereavement, Jury Duty

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/time-off-types/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Flexible Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Overview

GitLab offers a Flexible PTO policy that empowers team members to take the time they need while ensuring business continuity.

- As long as you have coordinated with your manager about your planned time off and made a coverage plan, you are free to take PTO.
- **GitLab does not set a specific limit on the amount of PTO per year.** However, for time off exceeding **25 consecutive calendar days** (including weekends and holidays), special permission is required from your Manager, People Business Partner, and the Absence Management Team at `leaves@gitlab.com`.
- Team members **may not substitute PTO for sick time**. If taking time off for medical needs (physical or mental health), follow the Sick Time Policy.
- **GitLab strongly recommends taking at least two consecutive weeks of time off, and a minimum of 25 days total per year.**

### PTO Protocols

- Discuss planned time off with your manager first and provide as much notice as possible, but **at least twice as many days as your planned time off** (e.g., **6 business days notice for 3 days off**).
- Coordinate with your team and stakeholders, especially during popular holiday periods.
- If a manager needs to request changes to your PTO plans, they will do so within **48 hours** and specify the goal or result impacted. If no compromise can be reached, escalate to the manager's manager.

### How to Enter PTO in Workday

1. Enter PTO directly into the Workday app, or through the Workday Slack integration.
2. Select day(s) → Click "Time-Off" → Click "Paid Time Off" → (Optional: add a comment) → Click "Submit".

**All team members must log their time away in Workday.** Entries can be edited up to **30 days in the past**. Older entries require a HelpLab submission to the People Connect team.

### Absence without notification

**Absence for 72 hours without notification could be deemed job abandonment.**

## Public Holidays

- All team members will see a public holiday accrual balance in Workday.
- Balance is country-specific, determined by local / contractual requirements, and reviewed annually against benchmarked National Holidays data.
- **Balance must be used within the calendar year; unused public holiday balances will not rollover.**
- Team members **choose** which public holidays to observe (Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging principle).
- Public Holidays in Workday are 1-day entries — partial public holidays cannot be taken.
- **Contractors of GitLab GSI** receive a standardized allocation of **12 public holidays** (the weighted average across GitLab countries).

### Mid-year hire pro-ration

(Days remaining in the calendar year ÷ 365) × Full-year public holiday entitlement, rounded to the nearest whole day (up if decimal ≥ .5, down if < .5).

Example: If the country entitlement is 10 days and you joined with 92 days remaining, balance = 10 × (92 ÷ 365) = 2.52 → **3 days**.

## Sick Time

- Covers personal illness (e.g., COVID-19, cold/flu, menstrual pain, surgery) and preventative care.
- For caring for a sick family member, use the **Caregiver Sick Time** policy.
- GitLab's Sick Time runs **concurrently** with any statutory sick leave.
- **Each team member may use up to 25 paid sick days** in a **rolling 12-month period**, available from first day of work.
- **Pay:** 100% of weekly wages for up to 25 working days. If a statutory benefit is available, you must apply for it; GitLab supplements to reach 100% of wages. Beyond 25 working days, leave is unpaid (subject to local requirements).
- Sick time is not paid out upon termination (except where required by local law).

### Reporting Procedures

**1–5 consecutive days:**
1. Notify manager.
2. Enter the time in Workday, label **"Out Sick"**.
3. Time auto-syncs to Time Off by Deel.
4. **No sick note required** for less than 6 consecutive days (unless local statute requires).

**6+ consecutive days** (considered a leave of absence):
- **USA:** Submit to **Tilt** (accessed via Okta). Personalized leave plan is built within **2–5 business days**.
- **All other locations:** Notify the Absence Management team via HelpLab, enter time in Workday as "Out Sick", provide evidence per local requirements.

## Caregiver Sick Time

- For time away to care for ill family member: parent(s), child(ren), spouse, domestic partner, grandparent(s), grandchild(ren), or sibling(s).
- **Up to 25 paid caregiver sick days** per rolling 12-month period, available from first day.
- Runs concurrently with statutory family-care leave.
- Pay mirrors Sick Time: 100% of wages (with statutory supplement) up to 25 working days.
- 1–5 days: notify manager and enter in Workday labeled **"Caregiver Sick Time"**.
- 6+ days: considered a leave of absence (Tilt for USA; HelpLab for others), documentation required.

## Bereavement

Bereavement falls under the Flexible PTO framework. Available from day one.

- **No set limit on bereavement days.** Continuous or intermittent.
- **Inclusive:** not restricted by relationship to the deceased — includes loss of pregnancy (birthing and non-birthing parents), family, friends, colleagues, and **pets**.
- **No documentation required** unless local/statutory law requires it.
- **Compensation:** 100% of weekly wages; health insurance continues on the same terms.
- **If bereavement exceeds 25 calendar days:** contact Manager, People Business Partner, and `leaves@gitlab.com`.

Request process: notify your manager, enter in Workday → "Time-Off" → "Bereavement", create a coverage plan. Support resources include **Modern Health**, **Bereave** benefits, and EAP.

## Volunteer Time

Volunteer time falls under Flexible PTO. Manager coordination with 2× notice, then enter in Workday under "Time-Off" → "Volunteer". GiveLab is GitLab's Team Member Volunteer Program.

## Legal Civic Obligations (Jury Duty / Witness)

GitLab provides leave for any team member summoned to judicial, administrative, or quasi-judicial proceedings (jury duty, witness).

- Available from day one.
- **Up to 25 full working days of paid leave** per authorized leave (unless local law provides otherwise).
- **Pay:** 100% of weekly wages. If absence exceeds 25 working days, Absence Management reviews case-by-case.
- Enter in Workday → "Time-Off" → "Jury Duty" and attach a copy of the summons (or email `leaves@gitlab.com`).
- Runs concurrently with other job-protected / income-replacement leave as required by law.

## Workday: Single Source of Truth for Absence

- **All team members and managers must log time-away requests in Workday.**
- Workday syncs to Time Off by Deel, which syncs to Slack (OOO + Do Not Disturb) and Google Calendar.
- Time Off by Deel does **not** sync with GitLab.com — for absences over 48 hours, update your GitLab.com status manually (profile picture → Edit Status, e.g. "OOO Back on 2025-04-28").
- Managers with delegated approvals should set up a delegate in Workday for prolonged absences.
- Questions: HelpLab portal.
