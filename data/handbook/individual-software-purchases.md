# Individual Use Software Purchases

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/procurement/individual-use-software/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Individual Use Software Overview

Individual Use Software is software essential for executing job responsibilities and that is distinct from existing tools and platforms already owned or accessible to your team.

- An individual's **annual expenditure on Individual Use software must not surpass $5,000**.
- If the cost exceeds this threshold, or if multiple people on your team need access, follow the full Procurement process for a new purchase request.
- **All software purchases charged to either personal or corporate credit cards will be DENIED for reimbursement.** You must follow the Individual Use Software process. Approved purchases are paid via a Navan virtual card once all approvals are obtained.

Refer to the Internal Acceptable Use Policy (`/handbook/people-group/acceptable-use-policy/`) for questions regarding freeware, or to GitLab's Mobile Device Policy for information on accessing GitLab resources via Okta on a mobile device.

## I Need Individual Use Software — Where Do I Start?

First check the list of **Tech Stack applications** (`https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/www-gitlab-com/-/blob/master/data/tech_stack.yml`) and the **Pre-Approved Individual Use Software listing** in the internal handbook.

- **If your vendor is listed in the tech stack:** Submit an access request using the Individual Bulk Access Request template at `https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/team-member-epics/access-requests`.
- **If your vendor is not in the tech stack:** Submit via the Individual Use Software Google Form (linked in the public handbook page) for appropriate routing.
  - Commonly requested software on the Pre-Approved Individual Use Software and Freeware list is subject to **expedited Security approval**.

Before pursuing a new vendor, consider whether one of our existing vendors can service your needs. Adding users to already-approved vendors:
- Saves GitLab money
- Decreases GitLab's threat landscape
- Expedites access

## What Happens When I Request Individual Use Software?

1. The Procurement team opens a GitLab Issue based on your responses and routes for necessary approvals, including your manager, Security, and Privacy.
2. Attach any relevant contracts, backups, or documents to the issue.
3. **Reviews may take several weeks**, depending on risk level, depth of review, and vendor responsiveness.
4. Once approved, the Procurement team issues you a **virtual one-time-use credit card via email** to purchase the approved software license at the approved price.
5. After purchase, forward a copy of your receipt to `ap@gitlab.com` if there are no other contracts or backup documents.

## Related Limits and Tools

- Cap: **$5,000 USD / year** per individual
- Physical Corporate Card (Navan) limit: **$5,000 USD** per transaction; annual $5k cap applies to the same Individual Use software rules (software purchases on the Navan Purchasing Card are explicitly **non-permissible**)
- Key tools: Okta, Zip (for over-cap requests), Navan (virtual card issued), HelpLab
- Slack channel: `#Procurement`
