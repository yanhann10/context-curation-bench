# Travel Safety, Security, and Insurance

**Source:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/travel/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-18

## Travel Safety and Security

Team member safety and security are a top priority for GitLab. If a team member feels uncomfortable or unsafe traveling to a location on GitLab's behalf due to concerns about the location, region, personal health, or other safety or security risks, please contact the People Safety & Security (PSS) team via HelpLab or email. The PSS team will help connect the team member with the right resources.

**Team member travel is not required unless deemed essential to the role. Please speak to your manager about whether the travel is mandatory and required for your role.**

## Trade Compliance Considerations

Although GitLab is an all-remote company that encourages working from anywhere, some places are subject to trade control restrictions. Team members **cannot** take company-owned devices to or otherwise work from US-embargoed countries or regions under any circumstances.

- **US-embargoed countries:** Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria
- **US-embargoed regions:** Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions of Ukraine
- **Additional restrictions:** Team members cannot take company-owned devices to or work from Russia or Belarus

This list is subject to change without notice.

### Emergency Assistance Program with International SOS

GitLab provides team members with comprehensive emergency support through International SOS (ISOS) during business travel worldwide. Your coverage is automatic and year-round.

**When to use International SOS:**
- Medical emergencies or illness during travel (including payment of emergency medical expenses)
- Claims assistance for the Business Travel Insurance Policy
- Safety and security concerns
- Pre-trip planning and health advice

**How to access ISOS services:**
1. Download the ISOS Assistance App
2. Register using your GitLab email address (unique password, not your GitLab credentials)
3. Press "Need Assistance" to connect to the nearest emergency center

You can also access a digital membership card at `https://myportal.internationalsos.com/WebPortal/MyOrganisation`.

### Business Travel Insurance Coverage

GitLab's Business Travel Accident Policy (underwritten by AIG) provides comprehensive protection for team members traveling domestically and internationally on business.

| Coverage Type | Benefits |
|---|---|
| Accidental Death | 5× Annual Salary (up to USD 500,000) |
| Emergency Medical | Up to $250,000 per occurrence |
| Security Evacuation | Includes natural disaster evacuation |
| Personal Deviation | Extended coverage for personal travel (25% of business trip length) |
| Trip Duration | Covered for trips less than 180 days |
| Baggage Protection | Maximum of $2,500 |
| Family Coverage | Includes spouse, domestic partners, and children under 26 when traveling with you |

This policy is primary and will pay before any personal accident policies.

### Using the Policy

**If you need medical assistance while traveling:**
1. Contact International SOS immediately (app, phone, or email)
2. ISOS will arrange appropriate medical care
3. ISOS handles payments directly with providers
4. ISOS submits insurance claims to AIG on your behalf

**If you've already paid for medical care during business travel:** Follow the claims guide to submit your claim. ISOS can help with claim instructions.

### Visa Letter Documentation

For team members needing confirmation of coverage for visa applications, complete the AIG Visa Letter Form and follow the submission instructions on the form.

## Secure Your Data During Travels

- **VPN:** Use a VPN connection when on untrusted networks to avoid Man-in-the-Middle attacks.
- **Devices in public places:** Use a privacy screen filter to prevent shoulder-surfing.
- **Speaking in crowded places:** Ensure that conversations about restricted data happen in secure locations.
- **1Password Travel Mode:** Use Travel Mode before entering risky countries or leaving devices unattended to ensure vaults will be safe if a device is compromised.

### If customs or border agents demand a password

If customs, border control, or law enforcement agents demand the password to a laptop or mobile device (company-owned): GitLab recommends you comply without protesting. After complying, follow the process to engage **Security on-call** as soon as you can safely do so. Even if you did not provide a password, if any device is permanently seized or temporarily removed from sight, engage Security on-call.

### Airport security checkpoint guidance

- **Completely power-off** laptops and mobile devices before any security checkpoints. This reduces attacks that target sleep-mode machines where disk encryption is not being enforced.
- Prior to the trip, remove any sensitive data from your local machine that is not absolutely required.
- For high-risk travel (e.g. conferences like Defcon), request advice in the `#security-division` Slack channel.
