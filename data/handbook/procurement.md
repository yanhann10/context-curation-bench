## What is Procurement?

The Procurement team manages GitLab's Supplier Lifecycle through strategically vetting and selecting vendors, negotiating commercial terms, purchasing goods and services, and managing renewals or vendor offboarding.

## Vendor Lifecycle Management

1. **RFP and Vendor Selection** — All new spend, changing of vendors for existing services, and market reviews every 3 years must follow the RFP process. An NDA must be in place before sharing confidential information with potential vendors.
2. **Negotiation, Privacy, Security, and Compliance Review** — Depending on spend and data types, Procurement, Privacy, Security, and Ethics & Compliance review the vendor.
3. **Contracting** — Work must not begin until a contract is in place. See the Authorization Matrix for signing authority.
4. **Vendor Onboarding** — Vendors must complete onboarding in Coupa to be paid.
5. **Recurring Vendor Reviews and Management** — Quarterly Procurement & Business Spotlights review upcoming renewals, cancellations, and new spend.
6. **Cancellation** — Terminations/non-renewals go through Zip with Procurement and Legal coordination.

## The Procurement Process

Procurement must be engaged **BEFORE** a purchase or work begins (unless the spend qualifies as a personal expense or meets a documented exception).

Start the Procurement Process:
1. Before agreeing to any business, legal, or pricing terms with a supplier.
2. Immediately upon receiving a contract or quote for new or recurring business.
3. When evaluating a group of suppliers or starting an RFP.

### How to start — via Zip

The majority of the procurement process lives inside **Zip**, accessed via the Okta home page (`https://gitlab.okta.com/app/UserHome#`). Submit a Zip Request for:

- Purchase Requests for renewals of existing vendors' services
- Purchase Requests for new spend **under $25k**
- Change Requests to existing POs
- $0 contract reviews including demos and trials
- Partner Revenue Payments
- Individual Use Software requests
- Termination / Non-Renewal Notices

### RFP thresholds (new spend or vendor change)

| Annual spend | RFP type |
|---|---|
| Over $250K | Procurement-led RFP, typically **5+ vendor bids** |
| $100K – $250K | Business-led RFP, **2–3 vendor bids** required |
| $25K – $100K | Business-led quick-bid, **2 vendor bids** required |
| Under $25K | **No bid required** |

## Contingent Workers

The Contingent Worker Policy covers three categories: Staff Augmentation Workers (agency-provided temporary resources), Consultancy Services (third-party professional services), and Independent Contractors (used by exception only). The policy sets duration limits, country hiring guidelines, contractor extension processes, and background screening requirements.

For subcontractors for the Professional Services team, use the process for Revenue Partner Payments.

## Review Timelines

Requests can take **5 days to 3+ weeks** depending on factors including new vs. existing vendor, need for negotiation, data-sharing requiring Privacy/Security review, contract complexity, and vendor responsiveness.

### 1. Sanctions Screening (Ethics & Compliance): 30 minutes – 3+ days

- Uses Risk Rate tool automatically. Use the vendor's complete legal name.
- If Risk Rate detects a potential match or the vendor is in a high-risk country, it may escalate to Trade Compliance Counsel for manual review.
- Include the vendor's website or LinkedIn page in the Zip request.

### 2.A. Buyer Review (Procurement): 2 days

- Ensure contract documents are uploaded.
- The request dollar amount must match the contract value; correct billing codes must be selected.

### 2.B. New Supplier Onboarding: 2–3+ days

- Depends on supplier response time. After Procurement sets the vendor up in Coupa, the supplier receives an email requesting banking and tax information.
- Procurement **cannot** complete this on the supplier's behalf (SOX compliance).

### 3.A. FP&A Review: 2 days

- FP&A ensures the spend is within budget and the Billing Code is correct.

### 3.B. IT Review: 2 days (for new software or contractors)

- Requester should have the vendor complete the **IT New Software Questionnaire** for IT review.

### 4.A. Legal Review: 3 to 21 days

- **Existing vendors (renewals/upsells):** 3–5 days. Upload complete contract terms or link to the existing agreement.
- **New vendors:** 1–3+ weeks.
- Delays from vendor counsel extend the timeline.

## Exceptions to the PO Policy

If you have a **personal expense** or a purchase that meets documented PO exceptions, you do not need to engage Procurement. Otherwise, Procurement engagement is required.

## Escalation / Urgent Requests

If a request cannot meet the stated timelines and has specific and quantifiable business impact, follow the urgent-request steps (documented internally). Procurement and Legal will prioritize with justification.
